A MODERN WAY TO COOK · ANNA JONES

Charred spring onion & white bean stew

vegetarian weeknight one-pot
A warming bowl of soft white beans, charred spring onions and a slick of chilli oil — the kind of supper that asks almost nothing of you.

Ingredients

  • 2 × 400g tins of cannellini beans, drained
  • 2 bunches of spring onions, trimmed
  • 3 tbsp good olive oil, plus more to finish
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes · a handful of flat-leaf parsley
  • Zest and juice of 1 unwaxed lemon

Method

  1. Char the spring onions in a dry pan until blackened in patches, then roughly chop.
  2. Warm the olive oil, add the chilli flakes and let them bloom for a few seconds.
  3. Tip in the beans with a splash of water and simmer gently until creamy.
  4. Fold through the onions, lemon and parsley; finish with a generous slick of oil.
